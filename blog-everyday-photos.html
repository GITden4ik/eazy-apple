01 / PHOTOGRAPHY

Small details. Better photos.

A memorable photo often starts before you press the shutter. Wipe the lens with a soft cloth, look at the light and decide what you want the viewer to notice.

Try photographing the same subject from two or three angles. A step to the side can remove a distracting background; getting lower can make an ordinary scene feel completely different.

For your next photo walk, choose a simple theme: reflections, one colour or the small details on your street. Give yourself ten photos to tell that story.
