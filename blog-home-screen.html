02 / EVERYDAY IDEAS

A home screen with room to breathe.

Your home screen is a space you return to throughout the day. Start with the apps you actually reach for and give them a place that feels natural.

Group the rest by what you do: keeping in touch, creating or getting things done. A quiet wallpaper can help the apps and widgets you care about stand out.

Try living with one simple first screen for a week. Notice what you miss, what you never open and what deserves to move closer to your thumb.
