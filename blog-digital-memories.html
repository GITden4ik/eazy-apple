03 / DIGITAL LIFE

Give your favourite moments a home.

Photos can become a long stream of screenshots, quick snaps and moments you meant to revisit. Set aside a few minutes to pick the ones that still make you smile.

Create a small album for a trip, a person or an ordinary month. Add a short caption while you still remember the place, the joke or what happened just outside the frame.

At the end of the year, you will have a collection that tells a story. Start small: choose five favourites from this week and give the album a name.
